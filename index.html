Camilo León-Sánchez

Spatial Data Infrastructures | Urban data science

Post-doctoral researcher at Wageningen University & Research

My research covers various aspects of Geo-Information Sciences. Currely I am focused on the integration of behavioural and human-centred data into Spatial Data Infrastructures (SDIs); this envolves topics such as open data, data sovereignty, semantics and open (geospatial) standards. Previously I have researched on the use of open geospatial data and Semantic 3D City Models (S3DCM) to enhance energy applications.
