Camilo León Sánchez

Spatial Data Infrastructures | Urban data science

Post-doctoral researcher at Wageningen University & Research

My research covers various aspects of Geo-Information Science. Currently, I focus on integrating behavioural and human-centred data into Spatial Data Infrastructures (SDIs). This involves topics such as open data, data sovereignty, semantics, and open geospatial standards. Previously, I researched the use of open geospatial data and Semantic 3D City Models (S3DCMs) to enhance energy applications.
